# THE WORLD TODAY — Sunday 22 November 2026

A one-page newspaper front page of the world on Sunday 22 November 2026:
**36 stories · 7 regions** (Europe 8, Middle East 7, Asia-Pacific 7, Americas 5,
Africa 3, Oceania 3, World 3).

Three drawn scene heroes lead the page:

1. **NO DEAL — COP31 collapses in Antalya.** The final text, with no
   fossil-fuel language, is rejected by the EU and ~130 parties; the longest
   COP ever, 44 hours overtime. (CONFIRMED — CNN · Reuters)
2. **186 DRONES — Russia fires 186 drones at Ukraine overnight; 161 shot
   down or jammed.** A five-storey block in Dymer collapses: 1 killed,
   14 missing; strikes in 18 places. (REPORTED — Ukrainian Air Force · AP)
3. **RADARS HIT — Iran claims 2 US Patriot radars hit in Bahrain; Bahrain
   denies any military attack.** A civilian site in Sitra burns; 2 hurt,
   none killed. (CLAIM — IRGC · Bahrain MoI)

Below the heroes: 10 more illustrated stories, a world map with numbered pins
matching the story numbers, a 23-item "ALSO ON SUNDAY" list, and a DATE CHECK
box (wires read up to 23:00 UTC) that names the stories deliberately left out
because they happened on other days, plus what was still undecided.

## Certainty chips

Every card carries a chip: CONFIRMED = 2+ outlets · REPORTED = wires that
day · CLAIM = one side alleges, unverified · OFFICIAL = recorded result ·
FORECAST = not yet decided. Scenes are drawn illustrations, not photographs.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the front page, 1024×1024, drawn by `draw.py` (PIL).
- `draw.py` — draws `entry.png` from scratch.
- `main.py` — stdlib-only copy program that reproduces `entry.png`
  byte-identically (run `python3 main.py`; it writes `entry-copy.png` and
  checks its SHA-256).
